Privacy Policy

RikkaUploader · Last updated: 2026

1. Data we collect

RikkaUploader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server owned or operated by the author.

2. What the application does

The Application uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to publish videos to the account owner's own TikTok profile. The only data exchanged is what TikTok requires for that purpose (OAuth access token, video file, title, privacy level).

3. Local storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the device that runs the Application. They are never sent to any third party.

4. Third-party services

The only third-party service contacted by the Application is TikTok's official API. Please refer to TikTok's Privacy Policy for information about how TikTok handles your data.

5. Analytics & tracking

The Application contains no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no advertising SDKs.

6. Data deletion

Because no personal data is stored on the author's servers, there is nothing to delete on our side. Local tokens can be removed at any time by deleting the Application's configuration file on the device.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the repository owner at github.com/adop1344-bot.
